# Development log — October 2026

## 2026-10-28 — The repository

**Done:** The repository, with the project's conventions: `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the `adr`, `devlog` and `review` checklists, the templates. ai-platform is pinned at `platform/`.

**Decisions:**

Drawn from Luni Cloud, the preview, and from Shopify, Home Assistant and xiaozhi-esp32-server ([architecture](../architecture.md)).
- [ADR-0002](../decisions/0002-stack.md), proposed: Python, with LiveKit Agents for conversations and FastAPI for requests. LiveKit's turn detector has no Vietnamese, which the pipeline must make up for.

**Problems / unfinished:** No code yet. The repository is private for now.

It must handle Vietnamese, mixed with English, first, and more languages later ([ai-platform ADR-0002](https://github.com/trungnguyen278/ai-platform/blob/main/docs/decisions/0002-languages.md)).

## 2026-10-28 — Reviewed for growth

**Done:** The architecture, reviewed at the owner's request against a project that grows a lot ([Growth](../architecture.md#growth)):
- each module's data is its own, referred to by identifier only, and everything that grows with use carries its home;
- skills are MCP servers;
- devices' links are ports;
- events are versioned and consumed idempotently;
- keys rotate, and objectives are set before launch;
- the law on personal data is decided before real users; the products are for adults, not children.

The platform gained the decisions this rests on: compatibility, conventions, homes (ai-platform ADR-0003 to ADR-0005).

The owner will use the cloud alone first, and open it to others once it is complete. The architecture stays whole; [Phases](../architecture.md#phases) says what is built for one owner, and what waits for others.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-architecture.md) revised, still proposed.

**Next:** ADR-0003, the conversation pipeline on official free tiers, with the end of Vietnamese speech; ADR-0004, hosting and LiveKit's media.

## 2026-10-28 — The conversation pipeline

**Done:** The providers, checked on their own pages:
- Gemini API's free tier now includes the Live API's native-audio models, with Vietnamese among their 24 languages and switching language within a conversation. Their own voice-activity detection ends the user's speech, which LiveKit's turn detector cannot do in Vietnamese.
- LiveKit Agents runs Gemini Live through its Google plugin, with tools.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0003](../decisions/0003-conversation-pipeline.md), proposed: Gemini Live first, on the free tier, with a cascaded chain of official free tiers behind it (Gemini, Groq, Azure); metered, with one daily budget.

**Problems / unfinished:**
- Free tiers let Google use the conversations to improve its products. It is acceptable for the owner alone; ADR-0006 settles the terms before anyone else joins.
- The free tier's limits for the owner's project are to be read in AI Studio; Groq's free limits are to be confirmed on its page.

**Later the same day:** the owner asked whether rotating keys would stretch the free tier.
- Google applies limits per project, not per key, so more keys gain nothing.
- Several projects or accounts used to pass the limits would breach Google's API terms (§2(d)).
- ADR-0003 now says so, with the limits known so far. An hour of conversation is about 100,000 tokens heard; the daily limits, unpublished, are to be measured.
- The owner settled on one key of their own per provider, with every other provider's free tier behind it: [providers.md](../providers.md) lists them by stage, with the chains' order.

**Next:** ADR-0004, hosting and LiveKit's media; then the first code: the token endpoint and an agent that talks.

## 2026-10-28 — Hosting

**Done:** The UDP that WebRTC needs, which a tunnel to a home cannot carry, solved by moving media off the home machine:
- the cloud link becomes two channels: a control WebSocket always on, a LiveKit room only to talk ([ai-platform ADR-0006](https://github.com/trungnguyen278/ai-platform/blob/main/docs/decisions/0006-two-channels.md));
- media goes through LiveKit Cloud's free plan (5,000 participant-minutes a month: some 80 minutes of conversation a day), and everything else through a Cloudflare Tunnel.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0004](../decisions/0004-hosting.md), proposed. The owner chose to start on LiveKit Cloud's free plan. Its ceiling, about 33 to 80 minutes of conversation a day, is metered, and media moves to a self-hosted LiveKit when it bites.

**Next:** The cloud link 3.0 in ai-platform; then the first code: the control channel, the token endpoint, and an agent that talks.

## 2026-10-28 — Following the cloud link 3.0

**Done:** The cloud link 3.0 is published in ai-platform (`cloud/v3.0`), and `platform/` now pins it. A review found the cloud's documents still describing 2.2, in which each device stays in its own room and its tools are called over the room's data channel. They now follow 3.0:
- a LiveKit room for each conversation, in which the agent and the device exchange the turns and answers;
- every call to a device, the agent's included, goes through `devices`, on the device's control channel.

**Decisions:** ADR-0001, ADR-0002 and ADR-0004 revised to match, still proposed.

**Next:** The first code: the control channel, the token endpoint, and an agent that talks.

## 2026-10-28 — Decisions accepted

**Done:**
- The owner accepted ADR-0001 to ADR-0004.
- Before accepting ADR-0003, the owner asked what its privacy decision means. Google's terms for the Gemini API's unpaid quota (updated 2026-04-28):
  - Google uses what is sent, and its answers, to improve its products and its machine learning, and people may read them;
  - the terms ask that nothing sensitive, confidential or personal be sent;
  - paid use is kept out of all that.
- The owner chose the free tier for the whole of phase one, while the owner alone talks to the robot. ADR-0003 records it. Paid use costs about $0.005 a minute heard and $0.018 a minute spoken, some $7 a month for 20 minutes of conversation a day.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-architecture.md) to [ADR-0004](../decisions/0004-hosting.md), accepted.

**Problems / unfinished:** The Gemini API's terms also require users of 18 or more, and API clients not likely to be reached by younger people. A device in a home with children may be, so ADR-0006 settles it before anyone else joins.

**Next:** The first code: the control channel, the token endpoint, and an agent that talks.

## 2026-10-28 — Speech meant for the device

**Done:** The [pipeline](../architecture.md#the-conversation-pipeline) judges whether each turn was meant for the device. People talk around a device, and a television too, and the owner asked that this be told rather than accepted.
- The Live model's proactive audio is used where the model has it; Gemini 3.1 Flash Live does not.
- Otherwise, a check of what was said.
- A turn that was not meant for the device is dropped with `turn.cancel`, as the cloud link 3.0 allows. The device then neither answers it nor stays open for it (ai-robot ADR-0018, proposed).

**Next:** The first code: the control channel, the token endpoint, and an agent that talks.

## 2026-10-29 — Media at home, proposed

**Done:** [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-media-at-home.md), proposed. Devices at home would talk through a LiveKit server at home, and reach the cloud on the home network; LiveKit Cloud would carry only the media of devices away.
- The owner develops with a PC and the robot's Pi on one network, and pointed out that the audio need not leave it.
- Conversations at home then have no monthly cap and less delay, and their audio stays at home until the agent sends it to its model.
- TLS stays on every connection (SEC-1). The certificates come from Let's Encrypt by DNS-01 through Cloudflare, for names under the owner's domain that resolve on the home network. Development uses mkcert.

**Problems / unfinished:** The tunnel of ADR-0004 needs a domain on Cloudflare, and so do these certificates. Whether the owner has one is not known yet.

**Next:** The first code: the control channel, the token endpoint, and an agent that talks, with a scripted pipeline until there is a Gemini key. A development guide for the PC and the Pi.
